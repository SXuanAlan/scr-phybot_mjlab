Predicted pad force and moment limits inferred from single-unit bench tests. The pad comprises 3 x 4 identical, engaged units with equal stiffness, on a 74.6 x 96 mm center-grid span taken from the current simulation geometry. Positive Ft is rake shear, Fl is lateral, and positive Fn is tensile pull-off. Ml is peeling about the lateral axis, Mt is bending about the rake axis, and Mn is twist about the normal. Unit observations use the instantaneous terminal-event command fz_cmd_n, not the post-slip measured reaction or the first-release label. The protocol uses 10 N preload, sequential tangential loading, and a normal staircase with 0.5 N increments and 3 s dwell. All 275 detached_normal trials are included in cell medians; 20 pre-normal failures are counted separately, and 1 hold(s) at the test ceiling are right-censored. This normal boundary is conditional on reaching the normal-loading phase. Piecewise-linear interpolation uses 69 sampled cells; there is no held-out model validation in this descriptive plot. The unmeasured negative-lateral half is mirrored and rendered as gray wireframe. (a) Zero-moment pad force boundary, Fn=N*g(Ft/N,Fl/N); black dots are scaled unit medians, not pad measurements. The triangle is a scaled unit censored hold, not a demonstrated pad load. (b) Force-normal sections under peeling moments. (c) Individual moment limits at Fn=Fl=0. Hollow triangles indicate still-admissible loads at the edge of unit-data support, not observed failure limits. (d) Coupled peeling/rolling limits. The rigid equal-stiffness distribution satisfies sum fi=F and sum ri cross fi=M; the first unit reaching its terminal characteristic boundary limits the pad. No extrapolation beyond the sampled force domain or below the 10 N unit compression preload is drawn. Consequently the surfaces are open and their plotted edges do not identify ultimate shear capacities. The characteristic unit median is not a median pad failure load or a pad safety/reliability quantile. The prediction assumes mount-compatible unit behavior and does not validate pad load sharing, progressive failure, or transient behavior on hardware.
