The figures reuse the original pad figures' layout, fonts, colors, and views, but use the empirical single-unit terminal-command cell medians and equal-stiffness pad model recorded in fig_pad_limit_surface_empirical.audit.json. The data, cell statistic, and interpolation have not been changed or smoothed. All pad boundaries are characteristic predictions, not pad hardware measurements or reliability bounds. Negative lateral forces assume mirror symmetry. Curves/sheets are truncated where the unit force data no longer constrain the model; these open ends are not ultimate capacity limits. No old Wang-model GPU validation points are overlaid. (a) Force-normal boundaries at peeling moments 0, 0.35, 0.7, and 1.05 N*m; all other lateral loads and moments are zero. (b) Individual moment capacities at zero normal/lateral force; open triangles mark data-domain lower bounds rather than predicted failure. (c) Bending interaction at Ft=70 and 200 N.
