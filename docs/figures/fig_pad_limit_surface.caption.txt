Fig. X. Equivalent whole-pad limit surface of a microspine pad under
per-unit moment billing. The hold envelope distributes the pad's 6D anchor
wrench (engage frame: t = rake/down-wall, l = lateral, n = outward normal)
onto a 3x4 grid of spine units spanning the physical contact patch
(74.6 x 96.0 mm, positions read from the robot model) with rigid-pad
equal-stiffness bolt-group statics, and judges every unit's 3D force
against its F0/12 share of the closed single-unit limit surface of Wang et
al. (IJRR 2017); the weakest unit releases the whole pad. Because the
distribution is linear in the wrench and the unit criterion is positively
homogeneous, the equivalent pad-level admissible set is computed exactly.
All capacities scale with the per-latch shear capacity draw F0 (domain
randomization: 400-650 N); plots use the 400 N floor. (a) Rake-tension
force plane at increasing pitch/peel moment |m_l| (bending about l, the
prying mode): with zero moment the per-unit test collapses exactly onto
the aggregate surface F0*R (max residual 2e-4, float32); moment shifts the
edge-row normal shares linearly, so the family nests at -40.2 N of
apparent tension per N*m (effective arm span_rake/3 = 24.9 mm) until net
compression f_n < -|m_l|/arm re-closes the envelope (curves merge into the
compression-free strip at the bottom). The 90-deg frame-0 hang load per
hand (70 N shear + 14 N tension) sits at occupancy 0.31, a 3.3x capacity
multiple at the DR floor. (b) Single-axis moment capacity versus
rake-shear preload: with no shear the pad holds almost no moment (0.19 /
0.21 / 0.78 N*m for pitch / roll / twist -- moment-induced edge tension
without shear falls on the closed forward lobe, which holds ~nothing),
while shear opens the tension lobe and capacity peaks at 1.19 / 1.27 /
8.65 N*m near f_t = 0.43 F0. Pitch and roll are nearly isotropic
(effective arms 24.9 vs 26.7 mm); twist is billed in-plane against the
much larger shear capacity (arm 36.3 mm against the corner unit's local
azimuth). (c) Pitch-roll bending interaction at fixed shear: corner units
accumulate both bending tensions, giving a near-linear (diamond)
interaction; contours at the hang shear (70 N) and at 200 N. All three
moment axes are sign-symmetric under the symmetric grid. The surface
assumes a fully seated, fully recruited latch (seat fraction and the
0.25 s recruitment ramp scale F0); the tilt-peel release (3.5-4.5 deg of
engaged-pad rotation) is a separate geometric channel outside this wrench
envelope, and pure compression rides the anchor for free. Generated by
scripts/plot_pad_limit_surface.py.
