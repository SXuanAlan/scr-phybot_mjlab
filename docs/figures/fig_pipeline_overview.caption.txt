Fig. X. Three-stage training pipeline for proprioception-only wall climbing
with hand- and foot-mounted microspine pads. (1) A teacher policy pi_T
(actor-critic MLP 256-128-64, 21-D joint-position targets at 50 Hz) is
trained with PPO to track a procedurally generated climbing reference: a
whole-body-IK four-beat regrip cycle (4.52 s at 50 Hz) whose dwells are
paced to >= 0.3 s so every fresh anchor outlives the 0.25 s recruitment
ramp. The reference enters three ways: as the mimic reward, as
reference-state initialization (uniform cycle phase), and as a 16-D
phase/contact-schedule command inside the observation. Training runs under
a staged wall-angle curriculum (45 -> 75 -> 85 -> 88 -> 90 deg) with the
constraint-based microspine contact model (per-pad 3x4 spine-unit moment
billing) and full domain randomization. Besides proprioception o_t, the
actor observes privileged simulator state x_t: per-pad spine engagement
(4), virtual-spine seating fraction (4), and base height (1); the critic
additionally sees per-unit anchor loads and hold margins. (2) A causal
estimator phi (MLP 512-256) regresses x_t from a 1 s history H_t of the
85-D proprioceptive frame (50 frames; contact/load-cell channels are
ablated, making the estimator pure proprioception). After an open-loop
warm start on teacher-driven rollouts, phi is trained with DAgger: the
frozen teacher acts on (o_t, e_hat_t), so labels are collected from the
estimate-in-the-loop distribution and the covariate-shift gap closes.
(3) With phi frozen, the policy (initialized from pi_T) is fine-tuned
with PPO for 600 iterations under the same reward and full randomization,
e_hat_t replacing x_t in the actor while the critic keeps the true
privileged state (asymmetric actor-critic); the finetuned pair matches the
truth-fed teacher. Deployment runs the (phi, pi) pair plus the reference
lookup table on proprioception alone -- no contact or force sensing; a
low engagement estimate triggers a debounced freeze of the reference
phase clock (3 beats to arm, 5 to clear, 1.5 s timeout) until the pad
re-seats.
