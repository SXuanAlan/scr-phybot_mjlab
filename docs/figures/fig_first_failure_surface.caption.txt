Normal force just before the first slip of a single microspine unit, from 231 bench trials (20260922-163807). Each trial applies a drag force Fx along the spines, then a lateral force Fy, then ramps the normal command from the preload towards tension; Fz is the measured normal force 80-20 ms before the detected first-slip onset (+ tension, - compression). (a) Target grid: filled circles are cell means coloured by Fz, crosses mark targets where a trial slipped during the drag or lateral stage, and grey open circles mirror the untested negative lateral half. The red line is the Fz = 0 contour of the mean fit; grey lines are its 2 N contours. (b) The fitted surface with the cell means and their sample SD; crosses are drag and lateral slips at the force they reached. (c, d) Sections at Fy = 0 and Fx = 0: cell mean and SD (none for single trials), the fit (red) and the residual spread of single trials (band; not a confidence interval). The fit is a degree-2 polynomial, even in Fy, over 52 cells holding 91 normal-stage trials; leave-one-cell-out RMSE 2.22 N against 2.91 N for a constant. It is drawn only inside the hull of the measured cells, is conditional on surviving the drag and lateral stages, and is not a validated failure envelope.
