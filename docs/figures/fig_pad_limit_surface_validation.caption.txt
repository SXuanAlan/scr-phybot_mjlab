Fig. X. GPU-rollout validation of the equivalent pad wrench limit surface
under per-unit moment billing: the three theory panels of the companion
figure (curves identical), overlaid with measured release and hold points
from 168 boundary + 56 interior wrench rays (one batched simulation, one
loaded pad, engage frame: t = rake, l = lateral, n = outward normal).
Boundary targets are sampled from each panel's theory curves — (a) the
rake-tension boundary at each pitch/peel moment |m_l| of the family,
including the reverse lobe and the compression re-closure branch, (b) the
three single-axis moment-capacity curves with alternating moment sign,
(c) the bending-interaction diamonds at both shear preloads — plus three
families for the 3D companion figure: a 48-ray zero-moment force
hemisphere (in-plane azimuth ring, capacity-lobe band, uniform tension
hemisphere) and two 12-ray f_t = 70 N slice families (force slice at
m = 0; twist-pitch ring). Each ray applies the 6D wrench proportionally,
s * w (the twist-pitch slice rays instead hold the 70 N shear preload
fixed and ramp only the moments, pinning their release into the exact
slice — the slice boundary's plateau-and-cliff direction sensitivity
would turn a ~1 % proportional-ray drift into a 10-25 % f_t overshoot). Because the per-unit
occupancy is positively homogeneous, the straight ray stays interior
until s = 1 exactly, so the anchor wrench at release (last-3-step median
of the constraint force and weld moment) is a measured boundary point.
Red points land on the theory surface with radial error |occ - 1| p50 =
0.012, p90 = 0.049 across all 168 rays (proportional families p50
0.010-0.015; the two-phase twist-pitch rays read p50 0.106 in 6D radius
because their release rides the cliff through the 3-step debounce, while
staying on the slice boundary by construction); the release-reason
taxonomy follows the worst spine unit's load direction (110
shear-dominant, 31 reverse, 25 lateral, 2 tension). Gray interior rays, ramped to s in
[0.3, 0.8] and held, stay latched in all 56 cases at steady occupancy
<= 0.75; points are plotted at their measured wrench, so an interior
point whose measured m_l differs from a panel's nominal slice may
visually overlap a neighboring family curve while remaining inside the
6D surface. Probe rig (envelope-neutral deviations from the deploy
stack): zero gravity, per-step velocity zeroing, F0 pinned to the 400 N
DR floor with recruitment ramp and seat-quality scaling disabled
(capacity scalings the theory pins at 1), tilt-peel disabled (a separate
geometric release channel), rotational anchor stiffened to torquescale
0.5 so commanded moments transmit through micro-rotations (the deploy
0.01 sheds couples by design; the bench 5.0 amplifies the rig's angular
jitter into one-step judged-moment spikes), release debounce 3 steps
against the rig's per-step impulse transients, and a 6D integral servo on
the measured anchor wrench (the exact quantity the billing judges) that
cancels parasitic load paths — feed-forward alone leaves a ~16 mm
effective pitch-moment arm that would hold every spine unit at the closed
forward lobe's edge and release pads at ~10 N regardless of direction.
