The figures reuse the original pad figures' layout, fonts, colors, and views, but use the empirical single-unit terminal-command cell medians and equal-stiffness pad model recorded in fig_pad_limit_surface_empirical.audit.json. The data, cell statistic, and interpolation have not been changed or smoothed. All pad boundaries are characteristic predictions, not pad hardware measurements or reliability bounds. Negative lateral forces assume mirror symmetry. Curves/sheets are truncated where the unit force data no longer constrain the model; these open ends are not ultimate capacity limits. (c) Upper/lower peeling-moment sheets versus twist and shear at Fl=Fn=Mt=0; these do not add artificial closing faces at data-domain boundaries.
